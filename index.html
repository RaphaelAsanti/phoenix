Question: How many Holy Angels of the Lord does it take to dematerialize an entire universe?

Answer: Only one — and only at the Command of God Almighty.

Why: Because Divine Authority is not discretionary authority.

Scripture

Deuteronomy 29:19-21

Moses warns that some people will hear the covenant and still convince themselves they are secure while choosing their own stubborn path. They speak a blessing over themselves even as they reject the instructions given to them. Moses explains that this self‑directed defiance harms everything around them. God’s response to that choice is unwavering: His rage remains against them continually, and He will never be willing to forgive them. The full weight of the covenant’s consequences will fall on them, their name will be erased, and they will be singled out for all the judgments described in the book.

That's the "Iron Rod" of doing things God's Way.

In the end, you're either a servant of your Lord, or a servant of someone else - perhaps even yourself.
