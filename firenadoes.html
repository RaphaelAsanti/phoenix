The Sign of the Transformation

Fire tornadoes and pyrocumulous clouds that form their own extreme fire weather are a recent phenomenon that scientists previously thought were impossible. There have been at least 9 of these events in recent years beginning with the Carr Fire in 2018.

Just as Elijah who came as John the Baptist was taken to heaven in a whirlwind, these serve as signs and warnings that the Hol is transforming to the Chol.

Documented Fire Tornado Events

  • 2018 Carr Fire (California): Generated an EF-3 pyrotornado with 143 mph winds that destroyed steel transmission towers.
  • 2019 Green Valley / Talmalmo Fire (Australia): Produced a violent vortex that flipped a 10-ton fire truck and caused a fatality.
  • 2019–2020 Badja Forest Fire (Australia): Formed embedded rotational mesocyclones inside a massive supercell smoke plume.
  • 2020 Kangaroo Island Fires (Australia): Spawned multiple cloud-tethered vortices that tore through eucalyptus forests.
  • 2020 Loyalton Fire (California/Nevada): Prompted the first official Fire Tornado Warning in U.S. history and produced an EF-1 tornado.
  • 2020 Creek Fire (California): Created explosive pyrocumulonimbus clouds and multiple simultaneous tornadic vortices.
  • 2020 Bear/North Complex Fire (California): Anchored a strong, shallow pyrotornado along its burning perimeter.
  • 2023 Gun Lake Fire (British Columbia): Verified as Canada's most thoroughly documented pyrotornado after crossing a lake.
  • 2025 Deer Creek Fire (Utah): Registered as an EF-2 tornado with 122 mph winds lasting for 12 minutes.
